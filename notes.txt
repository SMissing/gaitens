New Feature Ideas
-----------------

💡 - Staff Directory: A searchable directory of all staff members with their roles, contact info, and photos. Could include birthday reminders and work anniversaries.

💡 - Shift Swap System: Allow staff to post available shifts and request swaps with other team members. Managers can approve swap requests.

💡 - Performance Reviews: Digital performance review system where managers can schedule and conduct reviews, set goals, and track progress over time.

💡 - Staff Recognition Board: A public board where staff can give shoutouts/kudos to colleagues for good work. Could include categories like "Team Player", "Going Above and Beyond", etc.

💡 - Document Library: Centralized location for staff handbooks, policies, procedures, and important documents. Version control and read receipts.

💡 - Event RSVP System: For upcoming events, allow staff to RSVP so managers can track attendance and plan accordingly.

💡 - Staff Polls/Surveys: Quick polls or surveys for staff feedback on various topics (menu changes, event ideas, workplace improvements, etc.)


Gamification Ideas (To Make App More Engaging)
-----------------------------------------------

🎮 - Achievement Leaderboard: Show top achievers ranked by number of completed badges. Could have monthly/quarterly resets with special rewards for top performers.

tocl - Daily Check-In Streaks: Staff earn points/achievements for consecutive days of opening the app. Longer streaks unlock special badges (7-day, 30-day, 100-day milestones).

🎮 - Points System: Award points for various activities (completing training, submitting ideas, voting for EOTM, attending events, etc.). Points can unlock exclusive badges or be redeemed for rewards.

🎮 - Weekly Challenges: Managers can create weekly challenges (e.g., "Complete 3 training modules this week", "Submit 2 ideas", "Help train a new staff member"). Completing challenges awards bonus points/badges.

🎮 - Badge Collections & Sets: Group badges into collections (e.g., "Venue Master" - complete all venue knowledge badges, "Training Champion" - complete all training modules). Completing a set unlocks a special "Master" badge.

🎮 - Social Badge Sharing: When someone earns a rare achievement, it appears in a "Recent Achievements" feed that all staff can see, creating FOMO and motivation.

🎮 - Level System: Staff level up based on total points/achievements. Each level unlocks new features, badges, or profile customization options (colours, themes, profile frames).

lets do - Achievement Rarity System: Some badges are "Common", "Rare", "Epic", or "Legendary" with different visual effects. Rare badges create more excitement when earned.

🎮 - Team Competitions: Monthly team-based competitions (by venue or shift) where teams compete for points. Winning team gets a special badge or reward.

🎮 - Mystery Badges: Hidden achievements that staff discover by doing unexpected things. Creates exploration and surprise elements.

🎮 - Achievement Progress Dashboard: Visual progress bars showing how close staff are to unlocking the next badge in each category. Shows "X more shifts needed" or "Y% complete".

🎮 - Badge Showcase on Profile: Each staff member has a profile page showing their top 3-5 most impressive badges, total achievement count, and level. Others can view profiles to see achievements.

🎮 - Achievement Milestones: Special celebrations when staff hit major milestones (e.g., "50th Achievement Unlocked!" with a special animation/notification).

🎮 - Training Completion Streaks: Track consecutive training completions without letting any expire. Streaks unlock special "Dedicated Learner" badges.

🎮 - Idea Contribution Badges: Badges for submitting ideas that get implemented, most upvoted ideas, or being the first to suggest something that becomes popular.

🎮 - Event Participation Badges: Badges for attending X number of events, RSVPing early, or being part of special event teams.

🎮 - Referral/Recruitment Badges: Badges for helping recruit new staff members or successfully training new hires.
